3. Connected accounts

You can optionally link a Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, or Apple Music account. When you do, we request read-only access to your profile and your audience statistics on that platform, and we use it to populate your Music Mergers profile and momentum figures. We do not post anything, we do not read your private messages, and we do not access anything beyond what that platform's permission screen lists at the moment you approve it.

YouTube. You never sign in to Google for this. When you claim your artist profile, we look up the official YouTube channel that Chartmetric or Soundcharts lists for it and read that channel's public title and statistics (subscribers, views, video count) through YouTube API Services, to show your audience in the app and on your EPK. If you choose to verify the channel, we check its public description for a short code you add, which proves you run it; you can remove the code afterwards. We never post, upload, or read private data, and we do not share YouTube data with anyone. By using this feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to Google's handling of data. Stored YouTube data is refreshed at least every 30 days and deleted when you remove the channel in Settings, when it has not been refreshed for 30 days, or when you delete your account.

You can disconnect a linked account at any time in the app, which revokes our access going forward. Figures already calculated from it remain on your profile until you change or delete them.

4. Information we obtain from other sources

Music Mergers uses third-party music industry data providers, including Chartmetric and Soundcharts, for streaming, audience, and velocity figures about artists. This information is drawn from public and licensed industry sources rather than from your device, and it may appear on your profile and in the momentum and benchmark features whether or not you have linked an account. If you believe a figure attributed to you is wrong, contact us and we will correct or remove it.

5. How we use it

  • To operate the app and the features you use.
  • To authenticate you and keep accounts secure.
  • To deliver messages, offers, and notifications between users.
  • To determine what your subscription entitles you to.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.
  • To understand who uses the app, in totals by portal, age group, region, device and sign-up answers, and to see which sign-up screens lose people. These figures may appear in reports about the company; they never name you.
  • To send release tips, news and offers by email, only if you turned that on. Turn it off any time in Settings › Privacy & Data › News and offers by email.
  • To comply with law and to enforce our terms.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of a contract for operating the service, legitimate interests for security, diagnostics, and industry analytics, consent for the microphone, location, notifications, connected accounts and marketing email, and legal obligation where a law requires us to retain or produce something.

7. AI features

Several features send what you give them to a third-party provider so it can produce a result:

  • Cover Art Lab — your text prompt is sent for image generation, and generated artwork is scanned for potential copyright conflicts.
  • Dictation and audio in Writers Room — the audio you record is sent for transcription.
  • Industry Assistant — your questions, and a short summary of your own account (your stage name and genres, momentum score, recent releases and their status, upcoming shows, and how many split sheets or offers are waiting on you), are sent to generate an answer that fits where you are. The summary is built for each question and not stored.
  • Marketing and campaign plans — the release details you enter are sent to generate a plan.
  • Distribution — the audio of a release you submit is checked by Sightengine for AI-generated content, which distributors require.

We ask first. The first time a feature would send anything to one of these providers, the app names the provider and what will be sent, and sends nothing unless you allow it. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Data › AI features (Turn off AI features); each permission and withdrawal is recorded against your account.

These features run only when you invoke them, and only on what you submit to them. Generated output is stored against your account and is private to it unless you choose to share it.

We use these providers under their business and API terms, which do not grant them the right to train their models on the content we send, and we do not enrol in any programme that would allow it. We do not control these providers' own practices, and if their terms change in a way that affects your content we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

8. Location

Location is requested only by Studios Near Me, and only at the moment you open it. It is used to search for nearby recording studios and is not written to your profile, not attached to your account record, and not retained after the search. Declining the permission disables that one screen and nothing else.

9. Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used only while you are actively recording in Writers Room, or dictating lyrics. Recordings are stored in your library until you delete them. Dictation and audio processing send the recording for transcription as described in section 7. Nothing is recorded in the background, and nothing is recorded without you starting it.

10. Camera, photos and biometrics

The camera and your photo library are used only when you choose a profile image or cover art. On Android the app uses the system photo picker, so it receives only the images you select rather than access to your library. Images you choose are uploaded to your own profile storage and nowhere else.

Face ID, Touch ID and the Android biometric prompt are used only to unlock a sign-in saved on a device you have already used. The check happens entirely on your device. We never receive your fingerprint or face data — only whether the device accepted it.

11. How long we keep it

Deleting your account starts a schedule, not an instant wipe — the windows below are the same ones a real, scheduled job in our database enforces automatically once you request deletion:

  • Creative works in your Vault — deleted within 30 days of your deletion request.
  • Account and profile data — retained while your account is active, and for 90 days after a deletion request; at that point your login is disabled and identifying profile fields (name, email, avatar) are permanently scrubbed.
  • Messages and LOI threads — retained for 2 years after a deletion request for legal dispute purposes, then anonymized. This is also why a signed agreement or split sheet where another party is a signatory survives your own account's deletion — it is their record too, and its terms are untouched even as your own identifying details are removed from it.
  • Diagnostics (crash and telemetry logs) — 30 days.
  • Billing records — as long as tax and accounting law requires.

12. Your rights

You can request access to your data, correction, deletion, a portable copy, or that we restrict or stop a particular use. If you are in the EEA or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority. If you are in California you may request disclosure of the categories collected and their purposes, and deletion; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA.

Deleting your account: open Settings → Delete Account in the app. This starts the removal schedule described in section 11. You can also email aston@triplemgrp.com and we will action it within 30 days.

13. Children

Music Mergers is for people 16 and over and is not directed to anyone under 16. We ask for your date of birth when you first sign in and keep it private. We use it to confirm your age and to place your account in a broad age group (for example 18 to 24) for the totals described in section 5. Your date of birth itself is never shown to other members or shared outside the company. For users aged 16 or 17 we record the name and email of the parent or guardian who agrees to an agreement, and when they agreed. If you believe someone under 16 has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

14. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted by row-level security so accounts can only reach their own records, and entitlement is written only by our servers. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim otherwise; if a breach affects you we will notify you as the law requires.

15. International transfers

Our providers are primarily in the United States, so if you are outside the United States your data will be transferred there. Where required we rely on standard contractual clauses for those transfers.

16. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for a material change we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
