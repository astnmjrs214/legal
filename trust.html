How We Protect Your Music

Music Mergers, operated by Music Mergers Marketing Group.
Last updated 9 November 2026.

Music Mergers is where independent artists, songwriters and producers meet vetted A&Rs. Before anyone shares their work here, they should know exactly what happens to it. This page says it plainly. The binding versions are our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.

Your music stays yours

You own everything you upload: recordings, lyrics, artwork and documents. You give us only the limited permission needed to store your work, play it and show it to the people you direct it to, and that permission ends when you delete it. Releases you distribute stay live and keep paying you if your membership ends.

We never train AI on your music

Your uploads, recordings, lyrics and voice are never used to train AI models, by us or by anyone we work with, and we never sell or license them for that purpose. AI features such as Cover Art Lab, dictation and the Industry Assistant run only when you use them, only on what you give them, and only to produce your result. You can switch them off at any time in Settings › Privacy & data.

Every release is reviewed by a person

Before a release goes to stores you say whether AI was involved. Every release is then scanned and cleared by a person before it reaches our distributor. Fully AI-generated music is not distributed, and two false statements lock an account's distribution. Showcase songs, recordings and beats carry the same statement, and A&Rs see it in the member's own words.

A&Rs are vetted

A&Rs apply with a company email and are approved before they get in. A talent's deal contact details stay private until they accept a letter of intent, and are never shared for anyone under 18.

Your paperwork carries a fingerprint

A split sheet hashes its terms the moment each writer signs, so nobody can change what was agreed afterwards. Writer's Room sessions keep a tamper-evident record of who contributed and when, and every upload is dated by the server. Each member's creation record gathers all of it and exports as a PDF. It is evidence of when and by whom, not a copyright registration.

Your voice and likeness

Members can put on record that their voice and likeness may not be used to make synthetic performances without their written consent. Split sheets and beat licenses made in the app grant no AI training or voice-replica rights. A cloned voice or a deepfake can be reported in the app and reaches our copyright agent.

Copyright has a registered agent

Our copyright agent is registered with the U.S. Copyright Office as DMCA-1085230. Upheld notices count as strikes, and three strikes suspend an account. See our Copyright and DMCA Policy.

The only official app

The official app is MusicMergers by Music Mergers Marketing Group, bundle ID com.musicmergers.app, on the App Store and on Google Play. Our website is musicmergers.app. We never ask for your password, a payment or a fee outside the app's own screens and our Stripe checkout, and A&Rs never charge you to be heard. If an app, page or message claims to be us, do not sign in or pay, and tell us at aston@triplemgrp.com.
